# Silhouette support

## Get help

Open an issue at https://github.com/arfct/silhouette/issues. Include your macOS version, your camera model, and a screenshot if you can.

The app also has built-in help: choose Help > Silhouette Help.

## Setting up the virtual camera

1. Move Silhouette to the Applications folder and open it.
2. Allow camera access when asked.
3. Approve the camera extension in System Settings > General > Login Items & Extensions > Camera Extensions.
4. In FaceTime, Zoom, or another video app, choose "Silhouette" as the camera.

## Common questions

**The Silhouette camera doesn't appear in my video app.**
Check that the extension is approved in System Settings, then quit and reopen the video app. Some apps only scan for cameras at launch.

**Edges flicker or the backdrop shows through.**
Light the backdrop evenly. Then click the colour swatch and click your backdrop, widen Color Range slightly, and add a little Shrink or Stabilize.

**Green tint on hair or clothing.**
Raise Desaturate.

**Can I use it without a green screen?**
Silhouette is a chroma keyer, so it needs a green or blue backdrop. To try the app without one, choose Test pattern in the Source menu.
