Backgrounds

The Layers group has a Background box for what shows behind you and a Foreground box for what shows in front. Each takes a web address, a file path, or a hex colour such as #1E90FF. Their shared list holds None, the bundled Test page, your last ten entries, and Choose File…. Type a value and press Return, or pick from the list. Long entries show their end, which is the part that differs.

None

The keyed-out area is transparent. Most video apps show it as black; the preview shows a checkerboard.

Files

An image or a movie. Images load once; movies loop and decode in hardware. Files you pick are remembered and reopen on the next launch, and recent files reopen from the list. A path typed by hand works only for files the app may read; use Choose File… to grant access. You can also drop a file on the preview.

Colours

Type a hex colour, with or without the hash, in three, four, six, or eight digits. The last pair of an eight-digit colour is alpha, so #00000080 as a foreground is a half-strength darkening wash.

Foreground

The foreground is composited over the keyed camera and the background, so it needs transparency to be useful: a PNG with an alpha channel, a web page with a transparent body, or a colour with alpha. An opaque image or movie covers the whole frame. Web pages have no default white background in either layer, so a page covers only what it draws; the bundled test page is transparent and works as a foreground.

Web pages

A web page rendered at 1920×1080 behind you. Pages can react to face tracking; see Web page API.

Pages are snapshotted about 15 times a second while they change, so they cost more than an image. Static pages back off to 3 snapshots a second.

Fit

The output is always 1920×1080 at 30 frames per second. The camera and the background are scaled to fill it.
