Troubleshooting

The sidebar says "Not connected"

The camera extension is not enabled yet. Click System Settings…, enable Silhouette under Camera Extensions, and relaunch Silhouette if it does not connect within a few seconds.

"Move Silhouette.app to /Applications"

macOS only installs camera extensions from the Applications folder. Move the app there and launch it again.

Silhouette does not appear in Zoom or FaceTime

Quit and reopen the video app after enabling the extension. The camera is named "Silhouette".

The edge flickers

Raise Stabilize a little and add some Blur. If the backdrop is unevenly lit, widen Color Range slightly, then add a little Shrink.

Green tint on hair or clothes

Raise Desaturate.

A remembered video did not come back

The file was not readable at launch, for example not yet downloaded by a cloud service. Silhouette keeps the choice and uses a camera for this session; it returns when the file is available.
