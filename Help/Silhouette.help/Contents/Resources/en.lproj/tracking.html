Face tracking and the shadow

With Track face and eyes on, Silhouette runs Apple's Vision face detector on a small copy of each frame, on the Neural Engine. It finds the face box, head pose (yaw, pitch, roll), pupils, eye openness, nose, and mouth, about 15 times a second. The preview draws the box and eyes; the virtual camera output never does.

What uses it

  • Web pages receive every update through window.silhouette. The bundled test page shows the values and tilts a plane behind your head.
  • Vary with distance in the Shadow section scales the shadow with the face's size in frame. Slider values apply at a face 40% of the frame tall. Lean in and the shadow gets longer, softer, and lighter; sit back and it gets shorter, sharper, and denser. Turning it on enables tracking.

Cost

Tracking adds roughly 7% CPU on Apple silicon. Everything stays on the machine; no frames leave it.
