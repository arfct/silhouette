Getting started

1. Install the virtual camera

Silhouette must be in your Applications folder. On first launch it asks macOS to install its camera extension; approve it once in System Settings › General › Login Items & Extensions › Camera Extensions. The System Settings… button in the sidebar opens that pane. When the sidebar shows Connected at the top, "Silhouette" is available as a camera in other apps; click it for details.

2. Choose a source

The Source menu lists your cameras, a built-in Test pattern, a bundled Sample video of a green screen shoot, your last ten movies, and Video file… for using a new movie as the camera. Drop a movie on the preview to use it. The last source is remembered.

3. Set the key colour

Click Auto to sample the backdrop from the frame edges, or Pick and then click the backdrop in the preview; the preview shows the unkeyed image while you pick. Press Esc to cancel. Then tighten Range until the backdrop is gone and the subject is intact.

4. Pick a background

None leaves the backdrop transparent (black in most video apps, a checkerboard in the preview). File… takes an image or a looping movie. Web shows a web page and lists your recent URLs and the bundled test page. See Backgrounds.

The preview is mirrored like a mirror; the virtual camera output is not. View › Mirror Preview turns that off.
